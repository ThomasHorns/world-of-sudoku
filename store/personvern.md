World of Sudoku samler ikke inn, lagrer eller deler noen personopplysninger. Appen har ingen konto, ingen annonser og ingen sporing.

Appen lagrer følgende **bare på din egen enhet**:

- Ingen data sendes til utvikleren eller til andre.

## Barn

Appen samler ingen opplysninger fra noen, heller ikke fra barn.

# Privacy policy – World of Sudoku

Last updated: [PUBLICATION DATE]

World of Sudoku is made by [NAME], an individual developer.

## In short

World of Sudoku does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no account, no ads and no tracking.

## What is stored

The app stores the following **only on your own device**:

- games you have not finished
- statistics (times, number of solved puzzles, hints and mistakes)
- settings (language and whether auto-check was on last time)

This data never leaves the device. It is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## What does not happen

- No data is sent to the developer or anyone else.
- The app uses no analytics, ad networks or tracking.
- The app does not ask for access to the camera, contacts, location or other permissions.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If the app later gets features that require data, such as an account or online play, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.
